### Wnt signaling preserves progenitor cell multipotency during adipose tissue development (2023, Nature)

> ENA Accession: PRJNA815362
Library: Chromium Single Cell 3′ GEM Library & Gel Bead Kit v3

> Point to Remember: In SMART-seq2, cells remain identifiable because they are physically processed as separate libraries. In 10x, thousands of cells are processed and sequenced together, but their transcripts are experimentally tagged with cell-specific barcodes before pooling. Computational analysis then uses those barcodes to reconstruct which reads came from which cell, while UMIs allow the original RNA molecules within each cell to be counted without PCR duplicates inflating the counts.
